ISC Testing Stream Candidate — Controlled Prototype

AI-assisted trade compliance infrastructure for audit-ready export workflows

TPTN is a Canadian-built prototype designed to help government testing departments assess AI-assisted document processing, trade-rule validation, FTA eligibility logic, ESG workflow support, human-in-the-loop review, and audit-ready export records.

TPTN is a clickable prototype using fictitious or sanitized data. It is available for Government of Canada testing consideration through ISC Testing Stream Proposal ID T12-022084. No live government integration, production deployment, or executed pilot is implied.
Why This Matters
Canadian SMEs entering new export corridors often face fragmented portals, repeated documentation, unclear regulatory requirements, and limited internal compliance capacity. TPTN is designed to test whether AI-assisted workflows can reduce that burden while preserving auditability, human review, and public-sector trust requirements.

How It Works

How the ISC Testing Stream Pathway Works

TPTN is listed in the ISC Testing Stream pool as available for Government of Canada testing consideration. A federal department may identify an innovation from the pool and work through ISC to assess whether a controlled testing arrangement is appropriate. Testing interest does not imply procurement, adoption, endorsement, or production deployment. Section 2

What a Department Would Test

Six functional areas of the prototype are available for departmental assessment under a controlled testing arrangement.

01

Document Ingestion and Structured Data Extraction

AI-assisted parsing of fictitious or sanitized trade documents into structured, queryable data fields.

02

Trade-Rule Validation and Compliance Checks

Automated validation against HS codes, permits, restricted-party indicators, and documentation requirements.

03

FTA Eligibility and Tariff Logic

Free trade agreement eligibility assessment, rules-of-origin checks, and preferential tariff routing logic.

04

ESG Reporting Workflow Support

Structured workflow scaffolding for environmental, social, and governance disclosures linked to shipment records.

05

Human-in-the-Loop Uncertainty Review

Confidence thresholds route low-certainty outputs to a designated reviewer queue for human adjudication.

06

Audit Trail and Structured Evidence Export

Timestamped, tamper-evident audit logs and structured evidence packages suitable for regulator review.

Section 3

Low-Burden Government Test Scenario

A controlled, six-step assessment path that does not require integration with live departmental systems.

Department provides fictitious or sanitized trade documents.

TPTN ingests and extracts structured data.

AI-assisted logic checks HS codes, FTA eligibility, permits, restricted-party indicators, and documentation requirements.

Uncertain outputs are flagged for human review.

Audit records are generated with timestamped evidence.

Department reviews usability, traceability, accuracy, and testing metrics.

No live integration with Government of Canada systems is required for an initial controlled test.
