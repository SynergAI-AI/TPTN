CID-21 Paper Fallback
Converting ETR to paper-equivalent for legacy systems
📄 CAS-MLETR CID-21: Paper conversion with hash integrity. The electronic audit trail persists even when a paper copy is issued.
i
Not every jurisdiction is ready for fully electronic ETRs. The CID-21 paper fallback ensures TPTN works with ports, customs agencies, and buyers that still require paper documents — critical during the transition period.
Paper Conversion Available: You can convert this ETR to a paper-equivalent Bill of Lading at any point. The paper copy will carry a QR code linking to the ETR hash, and the electronic record will be marked read-only with a CONVERTED status banner.
